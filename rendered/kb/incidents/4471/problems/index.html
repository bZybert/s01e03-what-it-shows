The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap: “booked for 2 March, the invite says 3 February”. This is the bug #4471 names, and the fix it has queued.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY; booking-v2 prints DD/MM/YYYY. A day-first tenant’s people follow the hint, and the form reads what they typed the tenant’s way. The symptom is the same swap, but it comes from the form, so the parser fix does not touch it. It only reaches reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant.

one-day-move: the date moves by a day. No swap: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday”. That is a time-zone shift. The desk lists these reports apart in its notes and counts them under parser, where they are filed; this page counts them the same way and footnotes them under parser, with their own ticket.

How to tell them apart

  • Do not trust the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts). The two disagree often.
  • A swap through a booking-v3 form is hint. Any other swap is parser.
  • A date one day off is one-day-move, whatever the subject says.
  • One id is one report: the ticket export repeats some reports, and they count once.

What changes for the desk

  • Merge new reports into the ticket for their problem, not into the ticket with the same subject.
  • Keep #4471 for the parser. Close it only when the parser fix ships and the other two tickets are open on their own.
